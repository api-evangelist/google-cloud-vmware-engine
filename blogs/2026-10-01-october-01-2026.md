Feature Generally available : Bring Your Own License (BYOL) license management for Google Cloud VMware Engine is generally available (GA). BYOL license management lets you register and manage portable VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF) license keys in the Google Cloud console across projects associated with your Cloud Billing account. For more information, see License management .
